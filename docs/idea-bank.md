# Idea bank

For Edmonton, begin with the [six local scenarios](../edmonton/scenarios.md), then use this broader idea bank to extend your chosen workflow.

These are volunteer proposals, not organizer requirements. Pick a wedge that can be demonstrated with one input and one useful decision. Difficulty assumes a small team familiar with its stack.

| Idea | User and useful result | First demo | Difficulty | Main risk / validation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Application gap finder | Applicant sees missing evidence before sending | One incomplete synthetic application produces an actionable checklist | Low | Compare flags with an officer-reviewed checklist |
| Guided intake | First-time applicant understands the next question | Five questions become a structured draft | Low | Questions must reflect the applicable form |
| Citation inspector | Reviewer checks edition and source quickly | Select a claim and open its exact source location | Medium | Never treat a plausible article number as verified |
| Evidence matrix | Consultant tracks support for each claim | Link three claims to attachments and identify an unsupported claim | Low | Evidence presence does not establish adequacy |
| Reviewer handoff | Officer sees a concise package summary | Side-by-side application, unresolved gaps, and source excerpts | Medium | Measure time saved without hiding source detail |
| Revision diff | Applicant responds to requests for information | Two revisions show what changed and which gaps remain | Medium | Preserve history and reviewer ownership |
| Sheet inventory | Estimator sees missing drawing information | Classify supplied pages and report absent details | Low–medium | Confirm page labels manually |
| Concrete-first takeoff | Estimator confirms a slab quantity | Highlight dimensions, calculate volume, permit correction | Medium | Units, openings, scale, and thickness |
| Assembly quantity builder | Estimator turns reviewed geometry into materials | One wall assembly expands to quantities with explicit assumptions | Medium | Avoid overlapping geometry and duplicate layers |
| Factor match desk | Sustainability lead chooses a defensible factor | Compare three records with unit, geography, and scope | Medium | Product equivalence and dataset permission |
| Carbon hotspot explorer | Builder chooses where to investigate | Contribution chart plus one reviewed substitution | Low | Equal function and consistent system boundary |
| Plan-family tracker | Production builder reuses reviewed baseline | One base plan plus a documented variant delta | Medium–high | Mirroring and revisions can create double counts |

## Useful combinations

- Beginner codes team: gap finder + guided intake + evidence matrix.
- Strong document team: citation inspector + reviewer handoff.
- Beginner carbon team: manually reviewed quantities + factor match desk + hotspot explorer.
- Strong geometry team: sheet inventory + concrete-first takeoff; add carbon only after validating quantities.

## Other ways a volunteer can help

Create a permissioned sample case; facilitate a 15-minute estimator or officer interview; manually annotate a reference takeoff; document glossary terms; test an unseen input; review accessibility; check data terms; help rehearse a demo; turn feedback into reproducible issues. Capture reusable patterns without publishing team IP.
