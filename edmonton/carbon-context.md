# Embodied carbon in an Edmonton project

## Keep the challenge boundary explicit

The [event brief](https://edmontonunlimited.com/programs/built-world-challenge/) is the starting point for the residential carbon workflow. Preserve its A1–A3 product-stage focus in your prototype. Local context should improve material matching and usefulness without silently adding operational emissions or transport-to-site modules.

## Record local assumptions as evidence

| Context | Capture | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project location | Edmonton versus another metro municipality; selected plan/revision | Automatically inheriting City requirements across municipal boundaries |
| Supplier and product | Exact proposed product, plant/geography when available, specification and source | Assuming every material sold locally was manufactured locally |
| Assemblies | Actual slab/foundation, wall, roof, window and insulation specifications from the supplied plans | Substituting a generic national archetype for a reference takeoff |
| Product performance | Equivalent required function, thickness, strength/thermal or other relevant specification under expert review | Ranking incompatible substitutes solely by a lower GWP value |
| Factor evidence | Declared unit, modules, indicator/method, dates, geography and product/industry-average status | Calling a US or European average an Edmonton-specific factor |
| Climate/energy | Keep design context and operational-energy analysis separately sourced | Applying an invented Edmonton multiplier to A1–A3 carbon |
| Transport | Record future A4 inputs separately if explored | Including site delivery in a subtotal labelled only A1–A3 |

## Suggested first comparison

Use one authorized residential plan. Confirm slab/foundation quantities with an estimator. Present candidate concrete records that meet the relevant specification, units and methodology; retain match review. Show the effect of a quantity correction before a material substitution. Add framing, envelope and schedules as validated coverage grows.

Canadian or regional declarations may be more relevant than a distant generic dataset, but geography alone does not prove suitability. Use the [factor-matching method](../docs/factor-matching-method.md) and the actual declaration's limitations. Do not populate a table with invented local emissions values.

## Local sustainability context

The City's [green buildings page](https://www.edmonton.ca/city_government/environmental_stewardship/green-buildings-communities) discusses sustainability procedures for City buildings and embodied-carbon analysis. This is useful context for local demand and future applications. It does not establish a universal embodied-carbon assessment requirement for every privately built Edmonton home.

The City's [energy-code guidance](https://www.edmonton.ca/residential_neighbourhoods/energy-code-requirements) and [climate adaptation strategy](https://www.edmonton.ca/city_government/city_vision_and_strategic_plan/climate-change-adaptation-strategy) are adjacent context. Keep their roles distinct from a product-stage carbon estimate, and confirm current program-specific requirements before claiming compliance.
